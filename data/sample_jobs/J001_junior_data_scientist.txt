Job Title: Junior Data Scientist

About the Role
We are hiring a junior data scientist to join our analytics team. You will work with
product and marketing stakeholders to build models that inform day-to-day decisions.

Responsibilities
- Build, evaluate and document predictive models for churn and demand forecasting.
- Write SQL to extract and prepare datasets from our data warehouse.
- Communicate results clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
- Support the deployment of models into the production scoring service.

Required Skills
- Strong programming skills in Python
- Proficiency in SQL
- Solid understanding of Machine Learning fundamentals
- Hands-on experience with pandas and scikit-learn
- Working knowledge of Statistics and hypothesis testing

Preferred Skills
- Exposure to TensorFlow or PyTorch is a plus
- Familiarity with Natural Language Processing
- Experience with Data Visualization tools

Experience
0-2 years of experience. Freshers with strong project work are welcome to apply.

Education
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field.
