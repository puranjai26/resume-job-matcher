Position: DevOps Engineer

About the Role
We are looking for a DevOps engineer to own our container platform and deployment
pipelines as we grow from 20 to 80 services.

What You Will Do
- Operate and scale Kubernetes clusters running production workloads.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines used by every engineering team.
- Manage cloud infrastructure as code and keep environments reproducible.
- Improve monitoring, alerting and incident response practices.

Must Have
- 3+ years of experience in a DevOps or SRE role
- Strong experience with AWS
- Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes
- Proficiency in Linux administration
- Experience building CI/CD pipelines

Preferred Skills
- Terraform or another Infrastructure as Code tool
- Jenkins or GitHub Actions
- Monitoring with Prometheus and Grafana
- Scripting in Python or Bash is a plus

Education
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field.
