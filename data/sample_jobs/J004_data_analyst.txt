Job Title: Data Analyst

About the Role
Our operations team needs an analyst who can answer business questions with data and
turn recurring questions into self-serve dashboards.

Key Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards for operations and finance stakeholders.
- Write SQL queries against the reporting warehouse.
- Deliver monthly KPI reporting and investigate anomalies.
- Document metric definitions so numbers stay consistent across teams.

Required Skills
- Advanced SQL
- Strong Excel skills
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Working knowledge of Statistics
- Clear Communication with non-technical stakeholders

Good to Have
- Python for automation is desirable
- Exposure to A/B Testing
- Familiarity with Data Cleaning best practices

Experience
1+ years of experience in an analytics role.

Education
Bachelor's degree in any discipline; Business, Statistics or Computer Science preferred.
